Trening

Trening is an iPhone app for logging strength and cardio workouts: templates, a weekly plan, history and progress.

Free for up to 5 training days a month; Pro removes the limit (subscription or lifetime access) — purchased in the App Store.

What it does

  • Works offline — it does not connect to the internet (purchases are handled by the App Store).
  • No account and no ads; no analytics or tracking.
  • Your data stays on your phone.
  • Templates, weekly plan, calendar, workout history and records.
  • Backup and CSV export to the Files app.
  • 35 languages; light, dark or follow-the-phone appearance.
