### Cytaty (≤ 1 zdanie na źródło i rolę)

- ALDB, ks-hpp drill 29: „Perform this drill in the same manner as the alternate leg bound, but increase the distance from side to side as well as forward upon landings.”; drill 27: „Begin by pushing off with the back leg, driving the knee forward and upward to gain as much height and distance as possible before landing.”
- Bench Jump, ks-hpp drill 3: „rapidly extend the hips and knees; quickly and explosively push off the ground, while blocking the arms, into a flexed landing position on the platform.”; ks-k2 18.30: „Land on both feet in a half-squat position, step down from the box, and repeat.”
- Box Skip, ks-hpp drill 30: „Facing the first box from about two steps away, assume an upright stance with one leg slightly behind the other.”
- Double Leg Butt Kick, ks-hpp drill 6: „Using a quick countermovement jump, extend the hips for vertical height and, upon full extension, tuck the toes up and pull the heels upward and slightly backward into the buttocks.”
- Lateral Box Jump, ks-k2 18.33: „Stand to one side of the plyometric box; get into a comfortable, upright stance with feet shoulder-width apart.” Różnica: ks-k2 — zejście ze skrzyni i powtórka w drugą stronę; ks-nsca-podst — zeskok na drugą stronę, ciągle tam i z powrotem → w zdaniu tylko wskok na skrzynię.
- Side Hop-Sprint, ks-hpp drill 36: „Anticipate the landing on the last repetition; land in a sprint start posture and accelerate forward past a designated finish line.”
- Side to Side Box Shuffle, ks-k2 18.29: „Land with the opposite foot on the opposite side of the top of the box; this foot should land just before the ground foot.”
- Single Leg Butt Kick, ks-hpp drill 38: „Lift one leg by pulling the heel upward and only slightly backward to the buttocks.”
- Single-Leg Hop Progression, ks-hpp drill 39: „Completely balance on one leg while maintaining the off leg in a flexed position with the toes up, knee up in front of the body, and the heel up underneath the hip.”; ks-k2 18.16: „Land in the starting position and immediately repeat the hop using the same leg.” Różnica: ks-hpp zaczyna od lądowania z pauzą (progresja), ks-k2 od razu powtarza → zdanie bez tej różnicy.
- Single-Leg Stride Jump, ks-hpp drill 11: „Place the inside foot on top of the bench, and hold arms downward at the sides.”; ks-k2 18.28: „Stand to one side of the plyometric box with one foot on the ground and one foot on the box.”
- Star Jump, ks-hpp drill 5: „The difference is that you extend the limbs outward in all four directions away from the body.”

### Single-Leg Stride Jump — rozbieżność z rysunkiem (blokuje)

Rysunek (`scripts/figures/agent-pozy/plyo.mjs:19`, `:205`): skok jednonóż na skrzynię z lądowaniem na tej samej nodze. Obie książki: stoisz BOKIEM do ławki/skrzyni, wewnętrzna stopa NA niej, druga na podłodze; wybicie w górę stopą z ławki, powrót do tej samej pozycji. Zdania zgodne ze źródłami przeczyłyby rysunkowi → potrzebna zmiana rysunku (programista + recenzja L-047) albo ukrycie ćwiczenia. Do tego czasu zdania nie do aplikacji.
